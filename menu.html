How this board would work

Update today's soups from your phone in under a minute

  1. Open a private page on your phone. No laptop, no Google Doc.
  2. Flip on the soups that are on the stove. Pick from your own list of over a hundred, or add a new one.
  3. Tap post. The board on this page, the homepage and that location's card all change at once, with today's date on them.
  4. Sold out of Snow Crab at 12:30? Flip it off, and nobody drives across town for it.

Each location gets its own board, so Oak Ridge, Knoxville, Alcoa and Madisonville can each post their own soups without touching anyone else's.
